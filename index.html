Our Story

A Passion for
Exceptional Cuisine

Founded in 2009, Crave Co. was born from a dream to create a space where food transcends the ordinary. Our founder, Chef Isabelle Laurent, trained in Paris and brought her love for classic techniques back home to craft a uniquely modern menu.

Every dish tells a story — of the farmer who grew the vegetables, the fisherman who caught the seafood at dawn, and the chef who transformed these gifts into art on your plate. We believe in honest ingredients, bold flavours, and the magic that happens when skilled hands meet great produce.
