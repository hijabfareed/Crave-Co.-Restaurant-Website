Since 2009

A Labour of Love

Crave Co. was founded by Chef Isabelle Laurent — a culinary visionary who spent nearly a decade honing her craft in Paris, Lyon, and Tokyo before returning home to New York with a bold ambition: to build a restaurant that combined European classical techniques with the vibrant, diverse spirit of American food culture.

The name "Crave Co." was inspired by that primal, universal feeling — the deep, irresistible desire for something truly satisfying. From our very first service, we've been obsessed with creating dishes that guests not only enjoy in the moment but crave again and again.

Today, 15 years and eight awards later, we remain true to that original vision: honest ingredients, extraordinary craft, and the belief that a truly great meal changes how you see the world.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Everything we do is guided by a set of principles that have never wavered since the day we opened our doors.

Seasonal & Local

We source 90% of our ingredients from local farms within a 200-mile radius, working with nature's calendar, not against it.

Genuine Hospitality

Every guest is treated like family. We remember names, preferences, and milestones — because you're not just a table number to us.

Sustainability

Zero food waste is our goal. We compost, repurpose, and donate surplus daily. Our kitchen runs on renewable energy.

Continuous Learning

Our kitchen is a classroom. We invest in staff development and send our chefs abroad each year to bring fresh ideas back home.
